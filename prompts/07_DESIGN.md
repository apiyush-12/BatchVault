## Product feeling

BatchVault should feel:

- modern
- emotional
- youthful
- premium
- memory-first
- nostalgic without looking outdated

Avoid a generic enterprise SaaS dashboard appearance.

Photography and memories should be the visual center.

## Dynamic visuals

Use motion carefully for:

- photo stacks
- timeline reveals
- gallery transitions
- hover/focus states
- upload progress
- time-capsule countdown
- milestone celebrations
- throwback reveals

Do not animate everything.

## Important UX principle

The interface should emphasize:

people + memories + stories

not:

files + folders + technical metadata
